Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit (online grocery delivery) sales data using Microsoft Excel to uncover meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making. The analysis explores sales performance, customer behavior, delivery efficiency, payment trends, and product demand through interactive dashboards and pivot-based reporting.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze sales performance by product, city, and area Identify top-performing products and customers Understand delivery time patterns and their impact on customer ratings Study order trends by day and time to optimize inventory and staffing Evaluate discount usage and its effect on revenue Provide actionable insights to improve revenue and customer satisfaction

🗂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains customer order-level data with the following attributes: Order ID, Order Date & Time Customer ID, City, Area Product Name & Category Quantity, Price per Unit Total Product Value Discount Applied & Final Payable Amount Payment Mode Delivery Time & Delivery Status Customer Rating

🔍 Key Analysis Questions

Which product sells most frequently? What is the total revenue generated? Which cities and areas contribute the highest revenue? What is the Average Order Value (AOV)? How does AOV vary by city and day? Who are the top customers by revenue and order count? Which payment modes are most preferred? How does delivery status affect customer ratings? What days generate the highest order value?

📈 Analysis & Visualizations

The following analyses were performed using Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts: Product-wise total sales value City-wise and area-wise revenue contribution Order delivery status analysis Discount application trends Payment mode-wise revenue distribution Day-wise total order value Customer rating vs delivery status

Interactive slicers were used for: City Product Category Payment Mode Delivery Status

🛠 Tools & Techniques Used

Microsoft Excel Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts

Excel formulas: SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIFS IF, VLOOKUP / XLOOKUP Conditional Formatting Interactive Excel Dashboard

📊 Key Insights

Milk 1L is the top-performing product with the highest sales volume Weekdays generate 2.4× more revenue than weekends Only ~54.8% of orders are delivered on time, indicating delivery inefficiency Late deliveries strongly correlate with 0–1 star ratings Bangalore and Hyderabad are the strongest markets, with Bangalore alone contributing more than double the revenue of several other cities Wallet payments surprisingly exceed card payments, indicating strong digital wallet adoption

📌 Conclusion High demand for daily essentials like Dairy, Bakery, and Fruits Major revenue hubs: Bangalore & Hyderabad Biggest challenge: Delivery delays impacting customer satisfaction Payment behavior shows strong preference for digital methods (UPI & Wallets), while COD remains relevant

🚀 Final Output

An interactive Excel dashboard that enables stakeholders to: Track revenue and sales performance Identify operational bottlenecks Improve delivery efficiency Enhance customer experience
